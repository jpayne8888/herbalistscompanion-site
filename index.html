The materia medica

Meet the plant

Open an herb to meet the plant: its history, how herbalists have used it through the centuries, how that usage has changed today, and how you can use it to craft potent brews and delicious drafts of your own.

Every monograph is thoroughly researched and carefully crafted by hand to ensure you have reliable information, straight from the source.

The formula bench

Put the craft into practice

Put a few herbs together and watch the formula take shape as you build it.

  • The formula's actions, energetics and flavor profile are updated with each additional herb.
  • Customized step-by-step preparation instructions are presented for your particular herb set.
